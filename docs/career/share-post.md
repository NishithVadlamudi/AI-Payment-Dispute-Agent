# LinkedIn

Built Resolve: a payment-dispute demo where AI investigates fictional charges and humans keep refund approval.

The stack is Next.js, TypeScript, Gemini tool calls and Neon Postgres, with a private PostgREST adapter. Integer cents, database locks and unique execution keys protect the mock refund ledger.

Verification includes 194 key-free tests, 35 isolated Neon integration checks and a successful public Gemini investigation. Final explanations are rendered from validated payment records. The project documents its synthetic evaluation results and limits separately; it does not claim general financial-domain accuracy.

Try it: https://resolve-payments.onrender.com/payments
Source: https://github.com/NishithVadlamudi/AI-Payment-Dispute-Agent

No real banks or payment processors are connected. Launch video: docs/resolve-launch.mp4 (local asset). Narrated walkthrough: docs/resolve-demo.mp4.

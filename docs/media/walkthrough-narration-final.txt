Two matching charges. One purchase. Before a refund, someone needs to check the evidence.

Resolve helps investigate fictional payment disputes while keeping the final refund decision human.

Start with the dashboard. Choose a customer, inspect the payment history, and compare the two matching Northstar charges.

Matching records are a signal, not proof. Attach a payment to an investigation, then review your question before sending it.

The Gemini agent searches transactions, explains recorded decline codes, and proposes eligible refunds. It cannot approve them.

Next.js serves the interface and server. A private PostgREST adapter connects to Neon Postgres. Integer cents, database locks, and unique execution keys protect the mock refund ledger.

Explore the live demo. Investigate the charge. Keep the decision human.
